Forge and gear ​

The Forge opens after 1-1 Water Gate Steps. Your first forged weapon is always at least Rare.

Gear slots ​

  • Weapon, Hood, Coat, Gloves, Boots and the Spindle
  • The Spindle's line can be changed at the forge with Respin.

Rarities (low to high)

  • Plain → Fine → Rare → Heirloom

Forging ​

  • Recipes use raw materials: ore, creature hide, timber, flax and crystal, plus a little silver.
  • Short of something? The forge shows where to find it: a stage, the Gathering Hall, or a later chapter.

Forge tiers ​

  • Band 1: after 1-1
  • Band 10: after 1-5
  • Band 20: after 3-5
  • Band 30: after 5-5
  • Band 40: at Lv 40, once band 30 is open

Gear names by band ​

  • Band 1: Hushwood
  • Band 10: Ironbound
  • Band 20: Bellwood
  • Band 30: Thornbriar
  • Band 40 and up: Tempered

Binding ​

Gear binds to you the first time you wear it. The game asks first: Wear it?

Salvage and Thread Dust ​

Salvage gear you don't need in town for Thread Dust: 1 for Plain, 2 Fine, 4 Rare, 8 Heirloom. Gear can't be sold to shops.

Wayline Shards (from Lv 30) Shards thread extra power into your gear at the forge, with Thread Dust helping pay. You get them from boss stages, Hard elites, level rewards from 31 to 50 and the weekly prize.

More gear ​

Finish all three weekly goals for a Rare-or-better piece.
