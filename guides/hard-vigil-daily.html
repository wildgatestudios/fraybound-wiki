Hard mode, Daily and Weekly ​

Hard mode ​

Hard Adventure is the same road after dark: tougher foes, better spoils.

  • Opens after you clear 1-5 on Normal.
  • Each stage opens on Hard after its Normal clear.
  • Hard is its own chain: to play a stage on Hard, clear the stage before it on Hard first.
  • Play alone or with up to three fighters.

Daily challenge ​

Opens after you clear 2-1. Each day picks one of your furthest stages and adds a twist:

  • One life: no revives. Fall once and the run is over.
  • Double elites: every elite comes with a twin.
  • Beat the clock: clear it before time runs out. Win once a day for silver and materials. Days 3 and 7 of a streak pay extra. Miss a day and the streak starts over. New challenges come at 00:00 UTC.

Weekly goals ​

Open after you clear 2-5. Up to three goals each week, such as clearing a stage under par or on Hard. Finish all three for Rare gear. New goals every Monday.

Score, style and medals ​

  • Score: bigger foes give more points, and finishing under par adds a time bonus.
  • Style: use lots of different moves for a better grade (S is the best).
  • Stage medals: No knockouts, Style A+ and Hard par (beat the par time on Hard).

Coming later ​

The Vigil Road and the Wick Trial open in a later update.
