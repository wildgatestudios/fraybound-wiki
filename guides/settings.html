Settings worth knowing ​

Open More → Settings. There are four tabs down the side: Sound, Display, Controls and Language. Your choices are saved to your account.

Sound ​

  • Sliders for Music, Combat, Characters, Master, Ambience and Important alerts, plus Mute all.
  • Camera shake and Show my damage numbers on or off.
  • Graphics: Low or High. Low means fewer, lighter effects for a smoother game.

Display ​

  • HUD size, UI scale, Game text size (or Follow Roblox text size) and HUD opacity.
  • Soften scenery dims busy backgrounds behind the path. Easier on the eyes.
  • On touch screens: Control size, Left-handed controls and Follow-the-thumb stick (touch anywhere on the left to walk).
  • Reduce motion & flashing: no blinking, pulsing or sparkles.
  • Combat clarity calms busy fights. Your HP and boss warnings always show.
  • Colour vision and Performance mode.
  • Hold Attack to repeat (on by default).
  • Auto-potion uses a ready heal from quick slots 1–4 when your HP drops below the level you pick in Use below HP. Most players use 35%.

Controls ​

  • Keys: Arrow keys or WASD. 1–4 use items in both.
  • Keyboard hotkeys: rebind your eight action keys.
  • Arrange controls (touch): move and resize buttons or whole groups. Phone controls are new and may change.

Language ​

English, Español or Português (or Automatic), plus the text size options.

Found something confusing? Press Feedback in More. It goes straight to the team.
