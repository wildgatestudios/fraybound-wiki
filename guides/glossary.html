Glossary: Fraybound words ​

Fighter · your character. Class · Trailguard, Wayfinder, Runehand or Shadehand. Path · who your fighter becomes at Lv 30, picked when you make them (for example Bannerguard or Flintshot). Loomhaven · the home town between stages. Stage Gate · the door in Loomhaven that opens the chapter map. Stage · one short fight, a few screens long. Chapter · a road of five stages that ends with a boss. Sir Lackluster · the one who cut the threads to Loomhaven. Ragbob · one of Sir Lackluster's helpers. Loose End · the title you get when you wake up in Loomhaven. Hall of Memories · the shrines where you relive a champion's fight and take your vow. Vow · the Lv 30 step that turns your fighter into their path. Par · the target time for a stage's time star. Launcher · a hit that throws a foe into the air. Air Slam · a hit that slams a foe down. Parry · a guard timed just as a swing lands. Tatterlight · the meter that fills as you fight. Scrap · one of the four parts of the Tatterlight meter. Tatterburst · with Tatterlight full, Dash + Attack knocks every foe on screen flat. Tie Off · press Dash while a move is finishing. Spends one scrap. Link · after two Attacks on the ground, the skill marked LINK hits harder. Relay · a co-op catch: one fighter launches a foe, a teammate hits it in the air. Relight · how a teammate stands you back up in co-op. Hard Adventure · the same road after dark, with tougher foes. Drill post · short practice runs in Loomhaven. Sampler · the Loomhaven board with the fastest clears and biggest combos. Spindle · your sixth gear slot. Silver · the main money. Field Dressing · a healing item. Miles · how the Vigil Road counts. It opens in a later update.
