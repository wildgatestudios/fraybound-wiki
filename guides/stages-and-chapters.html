Stages, chapters and stars ​

The story so far ​

Sir Lackluster cut the threads that tie each place to Loomhaven, and every keeper went wild. Calm each boss to mend its thread.

Five chapters to play ​

  • Chapter 1, Canal Towpath (Lv 1-8): Water Gate Steps, Dyers Wharf, Lock Stairs, Millwheel Weir, Gooseneck Ferry Lock. Boss: Captain Gooseneck
  • Chapter 2, Fireflyfen Bayou (Lv 10-14): Reedwater Landing, Reedstilt Hamlet, Moonlily Pools, Drowned Paddle Steamer, Croakwell's Stilthouse. Boss: Granny Croakwell
  • Chapter 3, Rootbound Archive (Lv 16-19): Mossdoor Threshold, Candlewick Reading Hall, Inkspring Hollows, Leaflight Bindery, Hushwing Crown Loft. Boss: Mother Hushwing
  • Chapter 4, Ember Boilerworks Terraces (Lv 21-25): Rootmouth Tea Steps, Jadewater Pools, Whistleclock Row, Lava Kiln Bridges, Great Boiler Summit. Boss: the Steamkeeper
  • Chapter 5, Frostbell Peaks (Lv 26-30): Thawline Springs, Great Mug Inn Pass, Frozen Falls Bridge, Courtyard of Vows, Summit of the Bell. Boss: Old Yeti Yarn Chapters 6 to 12 come in later updates.

How stages open ​

Clear a stage to open the next one.

Boss keepsakes ​

2 on your first clear, and sometimes 1 on a repeat clear: Captain's Brass Button, Lily Brooch, Hushwing's Spectacles, Boiler Gauge Glass, Summit Bell Cord.

Stars ​

Each stage has three:

  • Clear the stage
  • Under the par time
  • Land the stage's hit combo Three stars pays more materials. Earn 15 stars (all of chapter 1 is enough) and forge a weapon to unlock the starlit look at the Forge.

The summit gate ​

5-5 Summit of the Bell only starts after you take your second vow. Your first Normal clear of 5-5 gives a band-30 armour kit: pick one piece to forge. You get one pick.

Lives: playing alone, you get 3 lives. A knockout stands you back up where you fell.
