ActiveLv 1Max rank 12Key Q

Stab one enemy within 7.5 steps ahead for 110% of your Damage at rank 1. Hold to repeat. Before your second vow, follow Needlecast on the same enemy within 2.5 seconds for a critical hit and 0.6 seconds off this skill's cooldown. With 3 Momentum, spends it to deal 25% more damage.

Duskskip ​

ActiveLv 12Max rank 12

Slip through enemies and turn toward those you passed. At rank 1, moves up to 8 steps, hides you from creatures for 1 second and makes your next skill hit within 3 seconds deal 30% more damage. Stops at walls and ledge edges.

MP6
Cooldown9s

ActiveLv 1Max rank 12Key W

Throw a star at one enemy within 16 steps for 110% of your Damage at rank 1. Before your second vow, follow with Precision Strike on the same enemy within 2.5 seconds for a critical hit and 0.6 seconds off its cooldown.

Damage110%
MP7
Cooldown5s

Smoke Blossom ​

ActiveLv 20Max rank 12

Create smoke around you for 5 seconds. At rank 1, covers 8 steps, slows enemies by 30%, makes creature shots miss 30% more often and grants you 15% evasion inside. At rank 12, also deals 40% damage each second to enemies inside.

MP8
Cooldown16s

Open Seam ​

PassiveLv 16Max rank 10

After dodging, using Duskskip or hitting an enemy from behind, your next skill hit marks it for 4 seconds. Your next basic attack on that enemy deals 150–300% extra damage. At rank 10, also restores 2% of max MP. Each rank grants 2 Guile and 1% critical damage.
